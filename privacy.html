Privacy & Data Handling Notice

1. Browser-Local Zero-Upload Conversion

Files staged in the Convert Now workflow on the main page execute entirely inside your local browser runtime using Web Streams and never leave your machine unless you explicitly trigger POST /v1/jobs in the Developer API Playground or submit an onboarding form.

2. Customer CRM & Marketing Opt-In

When you submit your name, email, company, contact details, or classic engine metadata via the onboarding form, those fields are stored in our SQLite CRM service (customers and marketing_leads_events). You can opt out of marketing communications anytime via POST /v1/marketing/unsubscribe or the one-click unsubscribe button in the Customers & Marketing tab.
